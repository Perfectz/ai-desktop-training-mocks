# Recording checklist

## Before recording

- Choose and save a deterministic scene URL for every shot.
- Replace sample names, prompts, projects, files, and responses with fictional lesson data.
- Confirm the product, scene, model label, and permission mode are correct.
- Hide bookmarks, notifications, password managers, and unrelated browser chrome.
- Lock the viewport, display scaling, and capture frame rate.
- Check text wrapping and horizontal overflow at the target size.
- Reload the URL and verify the scene returns to the same state.
- Keep the training-mock indicator visible when the footage could be mistaken for the real product.

## During recording

- Begin and end each take with a clean still frame.
- Move the cursor slowly and stop over the target before clicking.
- Do not enter real account, customer, or personal data.
- Capture an alternate clean plate without cursor movement.

## Before publishing

- Verify spoken instructions against the current licensed product.
- Add an “interface may vary” note for rollout-sensitive features.
- Check trademarks, third-party logos, and screenshots against the intended distribution license.
- Review the video frame by frame for real names, notifications, tokens, email addresses, or browser autofill.
- Save the repository commit and scene URL manifest with the project files.
